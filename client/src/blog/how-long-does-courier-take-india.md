> **In short:** most domestic courier parcels in India take **1–2 days within the same city**, **2–4 days between major cities** and **4–7 days or more to smaller towns and remote areas**. The actual time depends on the service (air or surface), the distance, the destination PIN code, weekends and holidays, and how quickly the parcel is picked up. The expected date shown in tracking is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Typical delivery times

These ranges are typical for ordinary domestic parcels. They're not promises from any courier, and individual parcels can be faster or slower.

| Route | Express / air | Standard / surface |
|---|---|---|
| Same city | 1–2 days | 1–3 days |
| Same state or nearby state | 1–3 days | 2–4 days |
| Between major cities (e.g. metro to metro) | 1–3 days | 3–6 days |
| To smaller towns and rural areas | 3–5 days | 4–8 days |
| North-east India, Jammu & Kashmir, islands, hilly areas | 4–7 days | a week or more |

"Days" here means working days after the parcel is picked up — not after you placed an order.

## What decides how long your parcel takes

### The service the sender chose

Express services move parcels by air between major cities, so long distances are covered overnight. Surface (ground) services go by road and are cheaper but slower. The same courier can deliver one parcel in two days and another on the same route in five, depending on the service.

### When it was picked up

For online orders, the clock starts when the seller hands the parcel to the courier, not when you ordered. Sellers often take a day or two to pack. If your tracking shows the shipment created but not picked up (for example "Manifested" on [Delhivery](/delhivery-tracking)), the delay is with the seller.

### The destination PIN code

Couriers deliver to big cities every day, but some rural or remote PIN codes are served only on certain days, or through a partner. Parcels to these areas often wait at a hub until the next vehicle goes there.

### Weekends, holidays and sale seasons

Many hubs and branches work fewer hours on Sundays and public holidays. During festival seasons and big online sales, hubs handle far more parcels than usual and everything slows down.

### Weather and road conditions

Heavy rain, floods, fog in north India in winter, landslides in hilly areas and road closures all add delays, especially to surface shipments.

### Delivery attempts

If the first attempt fails — nobody home, phone not answered, address unclear — the next attempt is usually the following working day. See [out for delivery but not delivered](/blog/out-for-delivery-not-delivered).

## Speed Post delivery times

India Post publishes target delivery times for Speed Post between cities on its website, and Speed Post reaches every PIN code in India — including many places private couriers serve less often. Items are sorted in sealed bags, so tracking updates can be less frequent. See the [Speed Post tracking page](/speed-post-tracking).

## How to read the expected delivery date

The expected or estimated delivery date shown by a seller or courier is calculated from the route and service. It's useful for planning, but it doesn't account for a missed pickup, a holiday at a hub or bad weather.

- **A day or two late** is common and usually sorts itself out.
- **No new tracking scan for more than 48 hours** on an ordinary route is worth asking about. See [parcel stuck in transit](/blog/parcel-stuck-in-transit).
- **Several days past the expected date** with no movement: contact the courier with your tracking number, and the seller if it's an online order.

## Getting your parcel faster

- **Choose express or air** when sending something urgent, and ask the counter which days it will travel.
- **Book early in the day.** Parcels booked after the day's pickup cut-off leave the next day.
- **Avoid sending on Saturdays** if it can wait — the parcel may sit at a hub over Sunday.
- **Write a complete address** with PIN code, landmark and phone number, so the first delivery attempt succeeds. See [how to write a delivery address in India](/blog/how-to-write-delivery-address-india).
- **Track it** on [Track My Ship](/) so you can respond quickly if the delivery person needs you.
