> **In short:** before the parcel ships, change the address in the seller's app or ask the seller. After it ships, the address on the label is fixed, but you can often still give the courier extra details (landmark, phone number, nearby address in the same area) or pick the parcel up from the delivery branch. A move to a different city usually means returning the parcel and sending it again.

## Why the address is hard to change once a parcel ships

When a parcel is booked, the destination PIN code decides which hubs it travels through and which branch delivers it. Every scan along the way is based on that route. Changing a few words in the address — a flat number or a landmark — is usually possible, but sending the parcel to a different PIN code means re-routing it through other hubs, which many couriers don't do for ordinary parcels.

So the earlier you act, the more you can change.

## Before the parcel ships

If your tracking shows **no scans yet**, or a status such as "order placed", "manifested" or "booked", the parcel probably hasn't left the seller.

- **Shopping apps and websites:** open the order and look for an option to change the address. Many apps only allow this before the order ships.
- **No option in the app?** Contact the seller's customer support. If they can't change it, cancelling the order and ordering again with the right address is often the quickest fix.
- **A person sending you something:** ask them to correct the address before they book the courier.

## After the parcel has shipped

### Small corrections in the same area

If the address is basically right but incomplete — a missing flat number, the wrong lane, no landmark — contact the courier with your tracking number and give the correct details. The delivery branch can usually pass them to the delivery person. Also make sure the **phone number** on the order is reachable; the delivery person will often call to find you.

### A different address in the same city

Some couriers can redirect a parcel to another address served by the same branch, or within the same city, especially if you ask before it's out for delivery. Others will only deliver to the original address. Ask the courier's customer care; if they can't change it, the options below may help.

### Collect it from the branch

If you can't be at the address, ask the courier whether you can **collect the parcel from the delivery branch** (for India Post, your delivery post office). You'll usually need the tracking number and an ID that matches the receiver's name. Ask how long they will hold it.

### A different city

Moving a parcel to another city usually isn't possible for ordinary deliveries. The usual route is to let the parcel return to the sender — for an online order, the seller will refund or reship it — and order again with the new address. If the parcel matters, talk to the seller first so the return is expected. See [what RTO means](/blog/rto-meaning-in-courier-tracking).

## Speed Post and India Post items

India Post has a **redirection** service for items when someone moves, which you can arrange at your post office. For a single item already on its way, ask your delivery post office or India Post customer care what's possible; if it can't be redirected, the post office will usually hold it for collection for a limited period after a failed delivery attempt. Our [Speed Post tracking page](/speed-post-tracking) explains the tracking updates.

## What to give the courier when you ask

- the **tracking number** (AWB / consignment number);
- the **receiver's name** as it appears on the parcel;
- the **current address** and the **corrected address**, with PIN code and a landmark;
- a **phone number** that will be answered on the delivery day.

Note the complaint or request number they give you, and keep checking the tracking on [Track My Ship](/) to see whether the parcel is moving to the right place.

## Avoid the problem next time

- Check the saved address in shopping apps before you pay. Old addresses are often selected by default.
- Write the address in full, with a landmark and PIN code — see [how to write a delivery address in India](/blog/how-to-write-delivery-address-india).
- If you're likely to be away, use an address where someone is always present, such as your office or a family member's home.

Watch out for messages asking you to pay a small fee to "update your address". Couriers don't do this by link — see [fake courier SMS and calls](/blog/fake-courier-sms-scams).
