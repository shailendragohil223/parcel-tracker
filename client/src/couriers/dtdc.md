## About DTDC

DTDC is one of India's oldest private courier networks, founded in 1990 and headquartered in Bengaluru. A large share of its parcels are booked at **franchise counters** — the small DTDC shops you see in local markets — rather than at company-owned branches. That matters for tracking: the counter that booked your parcel is often the quickest place to get help, and its name may appear on your receipt instead of "DTDC".

DTDC carries documents and parcels within India and abroad, with faster (air-supported) and cheaper (surface) services. The service you paid for decides how quickly the parcel moves, so two DTDC parcels between the same cities can take different times.

## Where to find your DTDC consignment number

- **You sent the parcel:** it is printed on the booking receipt from the counter, usually near a barcode. Keep the receipt until the parcel is delivered.
- **You bought something online:** the seller's "shipped" SMS or email, or the order page in the app, names DTDC and gives the number.
- **Someone sent it to you:** ask the sender for a photo of the receipt.

Enter the whole number, including the first letter (for example **C**1002935719 or **7D** followed by digits). Leaving out the letter is the most common reason DTDC tracking shows nothing.

## What DTDC tracking updates usually mean

The exact wording differs from parcel to parcel, but most DTDC updates fall into these groups:

| You may see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Booked / Softdata upload | The counter or seller has created the shipment. It may not have been handed over yet. |
| In transit / Dispatched to (city) | The parcel left a hub and is travelling to the next one. No scans appear while it's on the road. |
| Received at (hub or branch) | The parcel was unloaded and scanned at that location. |
| Out for delivery | It is with the delivery person for today's round. |
| Delivered | It was handed over. The receiver's name or a delivery OTP may be recorded. |
| Not delivered / Undelivered (with a reason) | A delivery attempt failed — for example, address not found, receiver not available or premises closed. |
| RTO / Returned | The parcel is going back to the sender. See [what RTO means](/blog/rto-meaning-in-courier-tracking). |

## Common DTDC tracking problems

**"No information" right after booking.** Franchise counters sometimes send their bookings to the DTDC system in batches, so a new number can show nothing for several hours or until the next day. Wait a day before worrying.

**Stuck on "In transit" for days.** Long road routes between hubs can show no scan for a day or more. If there is no update for over 48 hours, contact the counter where it was booked (if you're the sender) or the seller (if you bought it online). Our guide on a [parcel stuck in transit](/blog/parcel-stuck-in-transit) explains when a gap is normal.

**Undelivered because of the address.** Call the destination branch or DTDC customer care with the consignment number and give the full address with a landmark and a phone number that will be answered. A [complete Indian delivery address](/blog/how-to-write-delivery-address-india) prevents most of these.

## Who to contact

- **The booking counter** — for parcels you sent. It has the booking record and can raise a query with DTDC.
- **DTDC customer care** — contact details are on [dtdc.in](https://www.dtdc.in/). Have the consignment number ready.
- **The seller** — for online orders. The seller booked the shipment and can escalate it, replace the item or refund you.
