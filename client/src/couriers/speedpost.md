## About Speed Post

Speed Post is the express mail service of India Post, run by the Department of Posts under the Government of India. It reaches every PIN code in the country through the post office network, which makes it a common choice for official documents, exam admit cards, passports, legal notices and parcels to small towns and villages that private couriers serve less often.

The same tracking system also covers **Registered Post** and **India Post parcels**, so you can follow those here too with their article number.

## Understanding the article number

India Post article numbers follow an international format of **13 characters**: two letters, nine digits and two letters ending in **IN**, for example **EE123456789IN**.

- The **first letter** shows the type of service. Speed Post numbers start with **E**; Registered Post numbers usually start with **R**; parcel post numbers usually start with **C**.
- The **nine digits** are the item's serial number. The last of them is a check digit, so a single typing mistake usually gives no result rather than someone else's parcel.
- **IN** means the item was booked in India.

You'll find the number on the counter receipt, or on the label stuck to the envelope or parcel. Government departments and banks often include it in the SMS or letter that tells you something has been sent.

## What Speed Post tracking updates usually mean

| You may see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Item booked | The item was accepted at the post office counter. |
| Item bagged | It was put into a sealed bag with other items going the same way. |
| Item dispatched | The bag left that office for a sorting hub or the next office. |
| Item received | The bag or item was received at a sorting hub or post office. |
| Out for delivery / Item sent for delivery | The postman has it for today's delivery round. |
| Item delivered | It was handed over at the address. |
| Addressee absent / Intimation served / Door locked | Delivery failed and a notice was left. The item is usually kept at your delivery post office for you to collect. |
| Item returned / Return to sender | The item is going back, for example because it wasn't collected or the address couldn't be found. |

## Common Speed Post tracking problems

**Long gaps between "bagged", "dispatched" and "received".** Items travel in sealed bags, so individual items are not scanned again until the bag is opened at the next hub. A day or two without an update is common on long routes.

**"Intimation served" but nobody came.** This means the postman recorded a failed attempt. Take the intimation slip (if one was left), your ID and the article number to your delivery post office. Items are held there only for a limited period before being returned, so go soon and ask the counter how long they will keep it.

**Wrong or incomplete PIN code.** The PIN code decides which post office delivers your item. A wrong PIN sends it to the wrong office first. If you're expecting an item, check the PIN code with the sender; you can look it up on India Post's website.

## Who to contact

- **Your delivery post office** — for items waiting for collection or failed deliveries.
- **India Post customer care** — complaint options are on [indiapost.gov.in](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/). Quote the 13-character article number.
- **The sender** — for documents from a government office, bank or business; they can confirm the address they used.
