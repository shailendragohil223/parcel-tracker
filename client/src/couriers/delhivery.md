## About Delhivery

Delhivery is a logistics company founded in 2011 and headquartered in Gurugram. It handles a very large share of India's online-shopping deliveries, as well as business freight. If you ordered from a marketplace or a brand's own website, there's a good chance Delhivery carries it, even when the seller's packaging shows no courier name.

Because so many parcels are booked by sellers rather than by individuals, you'll usually get your Delhivery waybill number from the seller, not from a counter receipt.

## Where to find your Delhivery waybill number

- **Online orders:** look in the "shipped" SMS, email or WhatsApp message from the seller, or on the order page. It may be called the AWB, waybill or tracking number.
- **Parcels you sent through Delhivery:** the number is on the booking confirmation or shipping label.
- **Freight shipments:** business truck-load or part-truck-load shipments may use a different reference (such as an LR number). Ask the sender which number to track.

Delhivery waybill numbers are usually **13 or 14 digits** long.

## What Delhivery tracking updates usually mean

| You may see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Manifested | The seller has created the shipment and printed the label, but Delhivery hasn't picked it up yet. It's normal for this to last a day. |
| Picked up / In transit | Delhivery has the parcel and it is moving through its hubs. |
| Reached destination hub / At delivery centre | It's at the facility that serves your area. Delivery is usually close. |
| Dispatched / Out for delivery | It is on a delivery vehicle for today. |
| Delivered | It has been handed over. |
| Pending / Undelivered (with a reason) | The delivery attempt didn't happen or failed — for example, customer not reachable, address issue, or the delivery was rescheduled. |
| RTO (return to origin) | The parcel is going back to the seller. See [what RTO means](/blog/rto-meaning-in-courier-tracking). |

## Common Delhivery tracking problems

**Stuck on "Manifested".** This means the seller hasn't handed the parcel over yet. If it stays manifested for more than two days, contact the seller, not Delhivery — the parcel is still with them.

**Out for delivery but not delivered.** High-volume routes sometimes run out of time. The next scan usually explains why. Our guide on [out for delivery but not delivered](/blog/out-for-delivery-not-delivered) covers what to do.

**Delivery OTP.** Many Delhivery deliveries for online orders need an OTP, which the seller or Delhivery sends to the phone number on the order. Only share it with the delivery person **when you have the parcel in hand**. Never share it with someone who calls or messages you asking for it — see [fake courier SMS and calls](/blog/fake-courier-sms-scams).

**Wrong phone number on the order.** If the phone number is wrong, you won't get calls or OTPs and the delivery will fail. Correct it with the seller as early as possible.

## Who to contact

- **The seller** — first, for any online order. They booked the shipment and can raise a ticket with Delhivery, change some details, or arrange a refund or replacement.
- **Delhivery customer support** — contact options are on [delhivery.com](https://www.delhivery.com/). Have the waybill number ready.

Track My Ship is an independent tracking website and is not connected to Delhivery.
