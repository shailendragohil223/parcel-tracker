## About Blue Dart

Blue Dart is an express courier based in Mumbai and part of the DHL Group. It is known for time-sensitive deliveries and runs its own cargo aircraft alongside a road network, so many of its domestic shipments move by air between major cities.

Blue Dart offers both **air express** and **ground (surface)** services. A Blue Dart parcel sent by ground can take noticeably longer than one sent by air, even on the same route, so the expected date depends on the service the sender chose — not only on distance. Many online sellers use Blue Dart for higher-value orders.

## Where to find your Blue Dart AWB number

- **Online orders:** the seller's shipping SMS or email, or the order page in the app, usually names Blue Dart and shows the AWB (waybill) number.
- **Parcels you sent:** the AWB number is printed on the waybill copy you got at booking.
- **Business documents:** the sender's dispatch department can share it.

Blue Dart AWB numbers are usually **11 digits** long. Enter them without spaces or hyphens.

## What Blue Dart tracking updates usually mean

| You may see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Shipment picked up / Online shipment booked | The shipment exists in Blue Dart's system and has been collected, or is waiting to be collected. |
| In transit / Shipment further connected | It is moving between Blue Dart locations, often by air for express services. |
| Shipment arrived at (location) | It has been scanned at a hub or the delivery branch. |
| Shipment out for delivery | It is with the delivery person today. |
| Shipment delivered | It has been handed over; the receiver's name may be shown. |
| Consignee not available / Premises closed | A delivery attempt failed. Blue Dart usually tries again. |
| Address incomplete / incorrect | The delivery person couldn't find or confirm the address and needs your help. |
| Return to origin | The shipment is going back to the sender. |

## Common Blue Dart tracking problems

**Delivery needs an OTP or ID.** Some Blue Dart deliveries — often high-value online orders — need a delivery OTP sent to the receiver's phone, a signature or an ID. If you won't be there, ask the seller before the delivery day whether someone else can receive it.

**Not every PIN code is served the same way.** Blue Dart delivers to most of India, but remote PIN codes may be served less often, or only by ground services. Expect an extra day or two for these areas.

**Failed attempt because the phone wasn't answered.** Delivery people often call before arriving. Keep the phone number on the order reachable on the delivery day, and if a scan says "consignee not available", contact Blue Dart with the AWB number to arrange the next attempt.

## Who to contact

- **Blue Dart customer service** — contact options are on [bluedart.com](https://www.bluedart.com/). Quote the 11-digit AWB number.
- **The seller** — for online orders, especially if the parcel is returning to origin or is late.
- **The sender** — for parcels sent by a person or business; they are Blue Dart's customer for that shipment.
